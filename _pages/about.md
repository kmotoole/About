I am a PhD student in Northwestern University's [Technology and Social Behavior](https://tsb.northwestern.edu/) program, and work with [Agnes Horvat](https://agneshorvat.soc.northwestern.edu/) in the [LINK](https://link.soc.northwestern.edu/) lab.

My research investigates how technological affordances can facilitate creativity for both individuals and groups. I develop computational models of the processes involved in collective creativity and cultural evolution, and examine Human-Computer Interaction to identify ways in which technology can support creative expression and collaboration, with a particular focus on music.

Outside of my academic work, I am a classically trained flautist currently studying traditional Irish music.
